Can PlayStation detect the games?

Not as a reliable installed-game list through Sony’s public API. The lounge agent can sometimes read a currently running title when the local protocol exposes it, but PS4 and PS5 do not provide one dependable catalog scan for this use.

Recommended: manual catalogOwner adds titles once and controls which platforms and stations offer them.

Build rule

Failure policy

Every known edge case has a visible fallback.

01
Offline or timeoutKeep the last snapshot, label it stale, queue the command and retry with backoff.
02
Duplicate or late updateUse request IDs and version timestamps; process each action once and reject older state.
03
Invalid data or expired accessReject safely, preserve the session, re-authenticate and record the sanitized reason.
04
Partial checkoutNever end a session twice; reconcile the payment before issuing or retrying a receipt.
