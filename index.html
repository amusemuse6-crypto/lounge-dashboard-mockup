Can PlayStation detect the games?

Not as a reliable installed-game list through Sony’s public API. The lounge agent can sometimes read a currently running title when the local protocol exposes it, but PS4 and PS5 do not provide one dependable catalog scan for this use.

Recommended: manual catalogOwner adds titles once and controls which platforms and stations offer them.
Optional: active-title checkImport game names already reported by live station sessions. This does not scan installed libraries.
